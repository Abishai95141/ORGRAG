# PTO and clinic scheduling policy

## Paid time off

- Full-time staff accrue PTO at 6.15 hours per pay period (20 days per year) in years 1–3, and 7.7 hours per pay period (25 days per year) from year 4.
- Up to 40 hours of unused PTO roll over to the next year. Anything above that is paid out in January at your current rate.
- Halcyon observes 8 paid holidays. Staff who work on a holiday receive 1.5× pay plus a floating holiday.

## Requesting time off

Clinicians submit PTO requests at least 60 days in advance because patient schedules are booked up to 8 weeks out. Non-clinical staff give at least 2 weeks' notice. Requests are approved in order of submission; for the weeks of Thanksgiving and December 20 to January 2, approvals rotate year to year.

## Sick leave

Sick time is separate from PTO: 48 hours per year, available from day one. You do not need a doctor's note for absences shorter than three consecutive shifts.

## Clinic coverage

Every clinic must have at least one physician or nurse practitioner on site during open hours. If coverage drops below that, the clinic manager contacts the float pool first, then converts in-person visits to telehealth where clinically appropriate.

# HIPAA privacy and PHI handling policy

## Minimum necessary

Access only the protected health information (PHI) you need to do your job. Looking up records of family members, coworkers, celebrities or yourself through clinical systems is prohibited, even out of concern, and is a terminable offense. Patients can request their own records through the patient portal.

## Where PHI may live

PHI may only be stored in the EHR, the practice management system, and approved Halcyon systems listed on the intranet. Never put PHI in email subject lines, Slack, personal notes apps, or AI tools that are not on the approved list.

## Sending PHI

Use the EHR's secure messaging for patient communication. Email to outside providers must use encrypted email (type `[secure]` in the subject line). Faxes are still allowed for referrals but must use the cover sheet template.

## Breaches

Report a suspected privacy incident to the Privacy Officer within 24 hours by emailing privacy@halcyon.example or calling the compliance hotline. Examples: a misdirected fax, a lost device, or seeing a patient's chart open on an unattended screen. Breaches affecting 500 or more people must be reported to HHS within 60 days; the Privacy Officer handles this.

## Training

All workforce members complete HIPAA training during onboarding and annually by January 31. Access to clinical systems is suspended automatically if training is overdue by more than 14 days.

## Audits

The compliance team reviews EHR access logs monthly using automated anomaly detection. Unusual access triggers a review with the employee's manager.

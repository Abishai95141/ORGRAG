# Billing and insurance FAQ (for staff)

## Which insurance plans do we accept?

Halcyon is in-network with most major commercial plans, Medicare, and Medicaid in all states where we operate. The current list is in the practice management system under *Payers*. Out-of-network patients can be seen at the self-pay rate.

## What are the self-pay rates?

- New patient visit: $165
- Established patient visit: $120
- Telehealth visit: $79
- Annual physical: $195

Self-pay patients pay at the time of service and receive a 20% prompt-pay discount if they pay in full that day.

## What if a patient can't afford their bill?

Patients with household income under 250% of the federal poverty level qualify for the sliding-fee program, which reduces visit costs by 40–100%. Front desk staff can start the application at check-in. No patient is ever denied care because of inability to pay.

## How long do patients have to pay?

Statements go out 7 days after the insurance claim is processed. Balances are due within 30 days. Payment plans of up to 12 months with no interest are available for balances over $200. Accounts are never sent to collections without a final notice and a call from the billing team.

## Good-faith estimates

Under the No Surprises Act, uninsured and self-pay patients receive a good-faith estimate at least one business day before a scheduled service (three business days if scheduled 10 or more days out).

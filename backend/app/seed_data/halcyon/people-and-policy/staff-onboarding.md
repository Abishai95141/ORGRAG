# Staff onboarding at Halcyon Health

## Your first week

- **Day 1:** Badge photo, laptop setup, and the "Welcome to Halcyon" session covering our mission and care model.
- **Day 2:** HIPAA and privacy training (required before you get EHR access), plus workplace safety.
- **Day 3:** EHR training for clinical roles, or systems training for operations roles.
- **Days 4–5:** Shadowing at your home clinic with your preceptor.

## Credentialing

Clinicians must have active licenses, DEA registration where applicable, and board certification verified before seeing patients. Credentialing takes 30 to 60 days; your start date is set accordingly. Malpractice coverage is provided by Halcyon for all employed clinicians.

## Benefits

Medical, dental and vision coverage begins on the first of the month after your start date. Halcyon contributes $1,800 per year to your HSA if you choose the high-deductible plan. Clinical staff receive $2,000 per year for continuing medical education and paid CME time of 5 days.

## Your preceptor

Every new clinical hire is paired with a preceptor for 90 days. You'll have weekly check-ins for the first month and every other week after that. Your preceptor signs off on your competency checklist at 30, 60 and 90 days.

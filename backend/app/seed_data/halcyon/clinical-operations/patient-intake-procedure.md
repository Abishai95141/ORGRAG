# Patient intake procedure

**Applies to:** all Halcyon Health clinics and the telehealth front desk
**Owner:** Director of Clinical Operations

## Before the visit

New patients complete digital intake in the Halcyon app at least 24 hours before their first appointment: demographics, insurance card photos (front and back), medication list, allergies, and a consent-to-treat form. The front desk calls any new patient whose intake is incomplete 24 hours before the visit.

## Check-in

1. Verify identity with a government photo ID. For minors, verify the guardian's ID and confirm guardianship on file.
2. Confirm insurance eligibility in the practice management system. If eligibility fails, offer the self-pay estimate before the visit, not after.
3. Collect the copay at check-in. Patients with an outstanding balance over $250 are referred to the billing team but are never turned away from care.
4. Hand the patient off to the medical assistant within 10 minutes of check-in.

## Rooming

The medical assistant records vitals (blood pressure, heart rate, temperature, weight, and SpO2), reconciles the medication list, and completes the PHQ-2 depression screen for every adult at least once a year. A positive PHQ-2 (score of 3 or higher) triggers the full PHQ-9 before the clinician enters the room.

## Language and accessibility

Interpreter services are available in more than 200 languages by video within 2 minutes. Family members should not interpret for clinical conversations except in emergencies. Every clinic has at least one exam room accessible to wheelchair users and a height-adjustable exam table.

## Targets

- Door-to-room time: under 15 minutes for 90% of visits.
- Intake completion before arrival: at least 80% of new patients.

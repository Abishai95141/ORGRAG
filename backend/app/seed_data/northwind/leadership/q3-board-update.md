# Q3 2026 board update (confidential)

## Headline numbers

- Annual recurring revenue reached **$18.4M**, up 9% quarter over quarter and 41% year over year.
- Net revenue retention was 124%, driven by Enterprise expansion.
- Gross margin improved to 76% (from 72%) after moving runners to Graviton instances.
- Cash on hand: $31.2M, roughly 28 months of runway at the current burn of $1.1M per month.

## Customers

We signed 14 new Enterprise logos, including two Fortune 500 retailers. Logo churn was 2.1%, mostly Starter-to-Growth trials that did not convert. The largest risk is a $640K renewal in November with a logistics customer evaluating an in-house build.

## Product

Streaming joins enters private beta in October. The ingest outage in August (INC-2291) cost $58K in SLA credits; all action items are on track.

## Hiring

Headcount is 87, against a plan of 92. We are pausing two backfill roles in G&A to fund an additional solutions engineer in EMEA.

## Asks of the board

1. Approve the FY27 operating plan draft at the December meeting.
2. Introductions to data platform leaders at large retailers for the streaming joins beta.

# Expense and travel policy

## General rules

Spend company money as if it were your own. Submit expenses in Ramp within 30 days with an itemized receipt for anything over $25.

## Travel

- Book flights and hotels through Navan so we can find you in an emergency.
- Economy class for flights under 6 hours; premium economy is allowed for longer flights. Business class requires VP approval.
- Hotels: up to $250 per night in most cities, $350 in New York, San Francisco, London and Tokyo.
- Meals while travelling: up to $85 per day, no receipts needed for individual items under $25.

## Customer entertainment

Up to $150 per person for customer meals, including drinks. Alcohol is fine in moderation; it must never exceed half the bill.

## Equipment and software

Software subscriptions under $50 per month can be expensed with manager approval. Anything larger, or anything that will process customer data, must go through the security review in `#vendor-review` first.

## What you can't expense

Personal entertainment, fines and tickets, upgrades you chose for comfort, and gifts to government officials of any value.

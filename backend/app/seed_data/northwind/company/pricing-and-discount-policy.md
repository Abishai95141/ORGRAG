# Pricing and discount policy

This policy explains Northwind Pipelines' list prices and the discounts sales may offer without additional approval. It is reviewed every quarter by the CRO and CFO.

## Plans

| Plan | Price | Included events / month | Overage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | $0 | 5 million | Not available |
| Growth | $1,200 / month | 100 million | $8 per additional million |
| Enterprise | Custom, minimum $60,000 / year | Negotiated | Negotiated |

Annual prepayment on Growth earns a 15% discount (equivalent to $12,240 per year).

## Discount authority

| Discount off list | Who can approve |
|---|---|
| Up to 10% | Account executive |
| 10–20% | Regional sales director |
| 20–30% | VP of Sales |
| Above 30% | CRO and CFO jointly |

Discounts are calculated on the first-year contract value. Multi-year deals may add an extra 5% per committed year, capped at 10%, and this extra does not count toward the approval thresholds above.

## Non-profit and education

Registered non-profits and accredited educational institutions get 50% off Growth automatically. This is not a negotiated discount and needs no approval.

## Refunds

Monthly Growth subscriptions can be cancelled at any time, effective at the end of the billing period; we do not prorate refunds. Annual contracts are refundable within the first 30 days, minus usage at list price.

## Things sales may never offer

- Unlimited events on any plan.
- Custom data retention beyond 400 days without approval from the security team.
- Contractual uptime above 99.95%.

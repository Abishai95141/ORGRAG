# Welcome to Northwind Labs

Welcome aboard! This guide covers your first two weeks. Your manager and onboarding buddy will walk you through it, but you should be able to find everything here.

## Before day one

You'll receive a laptop (a MacBook Pro for engineering roles, MacBook Air for everyone else) shipped to your home address. Your Google Workspace account is created three days before your start date; you'll get the temporary password by text message.

## Week one

- **Day 1:** IT setup call at 09:30 your local time, then a welcome session with the People team. Set up 1Password, Slack, and two-factor authentication on every account — this is mandatory before you can access any production system.
- **Day 2:** Product deep-dive with a product manager. You'll build your first pipeline in the sandbox workspace.
- **Day 3–5:** Team-specific onboarding. Engineers ship a small change to production by the end of week one; this is a tradition, not a test.

## Week two

Shadow a customer call, read the last three postmortems, and schedule 1:1s with at least five people outside your team. Your buddy will suggest names.

## Benefits at a glance

- Health, dental and vision coverage starts on your first day.
- 401(k) with a 4% match, vesting immediately.
- $1,500 annual learning budget for courses, books and conferences.
- $500 one-time home office stipend, and $75 per month toward internet.
- Flexible PTO with a minimum of 15 days per year that we actually expect you to take.

## Where to ask questions

`#ask-anything` is the place for any question, however small. People, IT and finance all monitor it during business hours.

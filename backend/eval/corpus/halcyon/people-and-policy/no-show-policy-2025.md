# Halcyon patient no-show and late arrival policy (2025)

**Owners:** Clinical Operations and Revenue Cycle Management · **Effective:** March 1, 2025

Missed appointments cost other patients access to care: each no-show is a slot someone on the waitlist could have used. This policy sets out how front desk staff handle late arrivals and no-shows, consistently at every clinic.

## Late arrivals

- **In-person visits:** a patient who arrives **more than 15 minutes** after their appointment time is offered the next open slot that day with any clinician, or rescheduled. The clinician may still see the patient if the schedule allows, but the front desk does not promise this.
- **Telehealth visits:** the clinician waits **5 minutes** after the start time, then the telehealth front desk calls the patient once. If the patient does not join, the visit is marked as a no-show.
- Patients who are late because of an interpreter or accessibility issue on our side are never rescheduled for being late.

## What counts as a no-show

An appointment is a no-show if the patient does not arrive (or join) and did not cancel at least **24 hours** before the appointment time.

## No-show fee

- After the **second** no-show in any 12-month period, each further no-show is billed a **$25 no-show fee**.
- The fee is not billed to Medicaid patients or to patients in the sliding-fee program.
- Front desk staff may waive the fee for documented emergencies, with the clinic manager's approval.

## Repeated no-shows

- **Third no-show in 12 months:** the patient receives a letter explaining the policy.
- **Fifth no-show in 12 months:** the patient's record is referred to the regional medical director for a discharge review. Patients with an active pregnancy or an open course of treatment are not discharged.

## Reminders

Appointment reminders go out by SMS 3 days and 1 day before each visit. Patients can confirm or cancel by replying to the reminder.

## Reporting

Clinic managers review no-show rates monthly. The network target is a no-show rate below 8%.

# Halcyon clinic staffing standard (2025)

**Owner:** Clinical Operations · **Approved by:** Director of Clinical Operations · **Effective:** January 6, 2025

This standard sets the minimum staffing for each Halcyon clinic during open hours. Clinic managers use it to build the weekly schedule and to decide when to call the float pool.

## Staffing bands

Each clinic is placed in a band by its average in-person visits per day over the previous quarter, from ClearPath PM.

| Band | Avg visits per day | Physicians / NPs on site (min) | MAs per clinician | RNs on site (min) | Front desk staff (min) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Small | Under 45 | 1 | 1.0 | Not required | 2 |
| Medium | 45 to 90 | 2 | 1.25 | 1 | 3 |
| Large | Over 90 | 3 | 1.5 | 2 | 4 |
| Saturday session (any band) | — | 1 | 1.0 | Not required | 1 |
| Telehealth front desk | — | — | — | 1 (triage) | 1 per 40 scheduled visits |
| Float pool (network) | — | 2 on call daily | — | 1 on call daily | 1 on call daily |

"MAs per clinician" is the number of medical assistants scheduled for each physician or nurse practitioner seeing patients. Round up to the next whole person.

## Coverage rules

- Every clinic must have at least one physician or nurse practitioner on site during open hours. If coverage drops below that, the clinic manager contacts the float pool first, then converts in-person visits to telehealth where clinically appropriate.
- In small clinics without an RN, the physician or NP handles triage calls and the MA gives Tier 1 vaccines under standing orders.
- A charge nurse is designated whenever two or more RNs are on shift.

## When a clinic falls below minimum

1. The clinic manager calls the float pool.
2. If the float pool cannot cover, the clinic manager asks the regional medical director whether to reduce the schedule.
3. Any session run below minimum is logged in the staffing log for the monthly operations review.

## Review

This standard is reviewed annually by Clinical Operations with the clinic managers.

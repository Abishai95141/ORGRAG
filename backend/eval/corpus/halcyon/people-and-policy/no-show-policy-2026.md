# Halcyon patient no-show and late arrival policy (revised October 2026)

**Owners:** Clinical Operations and Revenue Cycle Management · **Approved:** September 23, 2026 · **Effective:** October 5, 2026

This revision **replaces the 2025 patient no-show and late arrival policy** in full. It follows a Q2 2026 review which found that no-show fees were rarely collected, fell hardest on patients just above the sliding-fee threshold, and had no measurable effect on no-show rates. Patients who miss visits most often told us the barriers were transport, work schedules and telehealth connection problems, so this policy puts the effort into removing those barriers instead.

## Summary of changes

- The no-show fee is **eliminated**. Halcyon no longer charges patients for missed appointments.
- Any no-show fees billed before October 5, 2026 that are still unpaid are **waived**. Revenue Cycle Management will reverse them on patient statements; front desk staff should not try to collect them.
- In-person late arrival threshold shortened; telehealth wait lengthened.
- A missed appointment now counts as a no-show only if the patient did not cancel at least 2 hours ahead.
- No patient is discharged from the practice for no-shows.

## Late arrivals

- **In-person visits:** a patient who arrives **more than 10 minutes** after their appointment time is offered the next open slot that day with any clinician, or rescheduled. The shorter threshold protects the door-to-room target for the patients who follow. The clinician may still see the patient if the schedule allows.
- **Telehealth visits:** the clinician waits **15 minutes** after the start time, and the telehealth front desk calls the patient twice during that time to help with connection problems. If the patient still does not join, the visit is marked as a no-show.
- Patients who are late because of an interpreter or accessibility issue on our side are never rescheduled for being late.

## What counts as a no-show

An appointment is a no-show if the patient does not arrive (or join) and did not cancel at least **2 hours** before the appointment time. Cancellations by reply to an appointment reminder count.

## Repeated no-shows

- **Third no-show in 12 months:** a care coordinator calls the patient to ask about barriers and offer help: ride vouchers, a telehealth visit instead, a different time of day, or an interpreter booked in advance.
- There is no discharge for no-shows. A patient may be discharged only for the reasons in the patient rights and responsibilities statement, reviewed by the regional medical director.

## Reminders

Appointment reminders go out by SMS 3 days and 1 day before each visit, and 2 hours before a telehealth visit with the join link. Patients can confirm or cancel by replying to the reminder.

## Reporting

Clinic managers review no-show rates monthly. The network target stays at a no-show rate below 8%.

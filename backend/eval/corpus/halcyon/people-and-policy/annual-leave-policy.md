# Annual leave policy (effective October 1, 2026)

**Owner:** People team · **Approved:** September 12, 2026 · **Effective:** October 1, 2026

This policy **replaces the "Paid time off" section of the PTO and clinic scheduling policy**. We now call paid time off *annual leave*, to match the language in our new HR system, RotaLeaf. Sick leave and clinic coverage rules are unchanged and still live in the PTO and clinic scheduling policy until that document is retired.

## What's changing

| | Old PTO rules | Annual leave from October 1, 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Accrual, years 1–3 | 20 days per year | 20 days per year (unchanged) |
| Accrual, year 4 onward | 25 days per year | 25 days per year (unchanged) |
| Carry-over into next year | Up to 40 hours | **Up to 56 hours** |
| Paid holidays | 8 | **9** (Juneteenth added) |
| Where to request | Email to clinic manager | **RotaLeaf** |

## Accrual

Full-time staff accrue annual leave every pay period: 6.15 hours per pay period in years 1–3 and 7.7 hours from year 4. Part-time staff accrue pro rata based on scheduled hours.

## Carry-over and payout

Up to 56 hours of unused annual leave carries over into the next calendar year. Anything above 56 hours is paid out in January at your current rate. The first carry-over under the new limit happens on January 1, 2027.

## Requesting leave

- Clinicians: at least **60 days** in advance, because patient schedules are booked up to 8 weeks out.
- Non-clinical staff: at least 2 weeks' notice.
- Requests are approved in the order they're submitted in RotaLeaf. For Thanksgiving week and December 20 to January 2, approvals rotate year to year.

## Holidays

Halcyon observes 9 paid holidays. Staff who work on a holiday receive 1.5× pay plus a floating holiday.

# Halcyon medical records and forms fee schedule

**Owner:** Health Information Management (HIM) · **HIM supervisor:** Joanna Kowalczyk · **Effective:** July 1, 2026

This schedule sets what Halcyon charges for copies of medical records and for completing forms, and how long each request should take. It applies at every clinic and to requests made through the telehealth front desk. All requests are logged in the HIM request queue in Meridian EHR.

## Patients' right of access

Patients have a legal right to copies of their own records. HIPAA allows up to 30 calendar days to respond; **Halcyon's standard is 10 business days** for every patient request that is not available instantly in the portal. Never refuse or delay a patient's request because of an unpaid balance.

Patients should be pointed to the patient portal first: most of the record, including visit notes, results and immunizations, can be downloaded there at no charge.

## Fee schedule

| Request | Requested by | Format | Fee | Turnaround |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Copy of own record | Patient or personal representative | Patient portal download | Free | Immediate |
| Copy of own record | Patient or personal representative | Electronic copy (secure email or encrypted USB) | $6.50 flat | 10 business days |
| Copy of own record | Patient or personal representative | Paper | $6.50 flat | 10 business days |
| Records sent to another treating provider | Provider, with patient request or for treatment | Electronic or fax | Free | 3 business days |
| Records for an attorney or insurer (signed authorization required) | Third party | Paper or electronic | $25 base fee plus $0.25 per page | 15 business days |
| FMLA certification form | Patient | Form completion by clinician | $25 | 7 business days |
| Short-term disability form | Patient | Form completion by clinician | $40 | 7 business days |
| School, camp or sports physical form | Patient or guardian | Form completion | Free if completed at the visit; $10 otherwise | 3 business days |
| Life insurance attending physician statement | Insurer | Form completion by clinician | $75 | 15 business days |
| Immunization record printout | Patient or guardian | Paper or portal | Free | Same day |

## Rules

- Fees for forms are paid before the form is released, not before it is started.
- Fees are waived for patients in the sliding-fee program.
- Sensitive records with extra legal protection (for example substance use treatment records) are released only after the Privacy Officer has reviewed the request.
- Requests from law enforcement or under subpoena go to the Privacy Officer, not to HIM staff.
- Faxes use the cover sheet template required by the HIPAA privacy policy.

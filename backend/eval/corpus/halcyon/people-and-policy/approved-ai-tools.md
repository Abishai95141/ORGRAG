# Approved AI tools for Halcyon staff

**Owners:** Privacy Officer and HIPAA Security Officer · **Updated:** October 5, 2026

The HIPAA privacy policy says PHI must never go into AI tools that are not on the approved list. This is that list.

## Approved

| Tool | What it's for | PHI allowed? | Conditions |
|---|---|---|---|
| Quillon Ambient | AI scribe that drafts visit notes from the conversation | Yes | Pilot clinics only (Riverside and Elm Park). Patient verbal consent at the start of every visit. Clinician reviews and signs every note. |
| Meridian draft replies | Drafts responses to patient portal messages inside Meridian EHR | Yes | Clinician must edit or approve before sending. Never auto-sent. |
| Halcyon Assist | Internal assistant that answers questions about Halcyon policies | **No** | Policy questions only. Do not paste patient details, even without names. |

## Not approved

- Public chatbots and general-purpose AI assistants, on any device, including personal phones.
- Browser extensions that read or summarize web pages.
- Meeting transcription apps (for clinical or non-clinical meetings that discuss patients).
- Translation apps for clinical conversations. Use the interpreter service instead (video interpreters connect within 2 minutes).

"De-identified" is not a loophole: removing a name is not enough to make a case non-identifiable, so treat any patient details as PHI.

## Requesting a new tool

Email the Privacy Officer (Margaret Holloway) and the HIPAA Security Officer (Ahmed Siddiqui). Any tool that will handle PHI needs:

1. A security risk assessment.
2. A signed business associate agreement with the vendor.
3. An entry in the clinical and business systems inventory.
4. For clinical use, a clinical safety review by the CMIO.

Typical turnaround is 6–10 weeks, mostly waiting on the BAA.

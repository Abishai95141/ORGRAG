# Halcyon clinic staffing standard (effective September 1, 2026)

**Owner:** Clinical Operations · **Approved by:** Rosa Delgado (Director of Clinical Operations) and Marisol Vance (Chief Nursing Officer) · **Approved:** August 10, 2026 · **Effective:** September 1, 2026

This standard **replaces the 2025 clinic staffing standard**. It was revised after the Q2 2026 operations review found that medium and large clinics were missing the door-to-room target on most afternoons, and that small clinics without an RN were sending too many triage calls to the physician.

## What changed

- Band thresholds are lower: small is now under 40 visits a day, large is over 80.
- More medical assistants per clinician in medium and large clinics.
- Every clinic, including small clinics, must now have at least one RN on site during open hours. Saturday sessions also need an RN.
- Large clinics designate a charge nurse on every shift.
- The telehealth front desk has two triage RNs and more front desk staff.

## Staffing bands

Each clinic is placed in a band every quarter using the "Avg visits per day" column in the clinic directory.

| Band | Avg visits per day | Physicians / NPs on site (min) | MAs per clinician | RNs on site (min) | Front desk staff (min) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Small | Under 40 | 1 | 1.0 | 1 | 2 |
| Medium | 40 to 80 | 2 | 1.5 | 1 | 3 |
| Large | Over 80 | 3 | 2.0 | 2, one designated charge nurse | 4 |
| Saturday session (any band) | — | 1 | 1.0 | 1 | 2 |
| Sunday session (Harbor Point) | — | 1 | 1.0 | 1 | 1 |
| Telehealth front desk | — | — | — | 2 (triage) | 1 per 35 scheduled visits |
| Float pool (network) | — | 2 on call daily | — | 2 on call daily | 1 on call daily |

"MAs per clinician" is the number of medical assistants scheduled for each physician or nurse practitioner seeing patients. Round up to the next whole person.

## Coverage rules

- Every clinic must have at least one physician or nurse practitioner on site during open hours. If coverage drops below that, the clinic manager contacts the float pool first, then converts in-person visits to telehealth where clinically appropriate.
- The charge nurse in a large clinic coordinates the immediate response to any patient safety event, as the sentinel and adverse event reporting policy describes. In medium and small clinics a charge nurse is designated whenever two or more RNs are on shift; otherwise the clinic manager fills that role.
- In every band, MAs give the Tier 1 items the in-clinic formulary lists as given by MAs, under standing orders.

## When a clinic falls below minimum

1. The clinic manager calls the float pool.
2. If the float pool cannot cover, the clinic manager asks the regional medical director whether to reduce the schedule.
3. A session run without an RN is reported in SafeReport as a near miss so QPS can track it.

## Transition

Clinics had until October 1, 2026 to reach the new minimums. The float pool was increased by two RNs to cover the gap.

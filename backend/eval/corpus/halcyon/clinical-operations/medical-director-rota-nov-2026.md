# Halcyon on-call medical director rota — November 2026

**Owner:** Office of the Chief Medical Officer · **Published:** October 19, 2026

The on-call medical director is the senior physician on duty for the whole Halcyon network, outside the normal chain of supervision at each clinic. The on-call medical director:

- receives critical lab results when the ordering clinician cannot be reached (clinical escalation protocol);
- is notified of sentinel events by the charge nurse (sentinel and adverse event reporting policy);
- may approve emergency changes to clinical systems (clinical systems change control);
- advises on transfers, unexpected clinic closures, and any clinical question a supervising physician can't resolve.

## How to reach the on-call medical director

Call the Halcyon answering service at **555-0144** and choose **option 2**. The answering service pages the on-call medical director and, if there is no callback within 10 minutes, pages the backup. Do not text physicians' personal mobile numbers; pages through the answering service are logged and timed, which is what the chart documentation needs.

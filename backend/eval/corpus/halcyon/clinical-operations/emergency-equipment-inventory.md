# Halcyon emergency equipment inventory (Q4 2026 check)

**Owner:** Clinical Operations · **Checks performed by:** each site's clinic manager or delegate · **Compiled:** October 26, 2026

Every Halcyon clinic keeps at least one automated external defibrillator (AED), an emergency cart and a portable oxygen cylinder. Sites are listed by their clinic directory code. This table lists every AED in the network, plus the carts and cylinders sampled in Clinical Operations' October spot audit.

## Monthly checks

- **AED:** self-test indicator is green, pads and battery are within date, and a spare pad set is present.
- **Emergency cart:** tamper seal number matches the log. A full restock check is done every 6 months by the clinical pharmacist.
- **Oxygen cylinder:** record the pressure; replace any cylinder below 500 psi.

Items marked **Action needed** must be closed by the site's clinic manager, who records the fix in the equipment log and tells Clinical Operations. An AED that fails its self-test is taken out of service the same day and replaced with a loaner from the Clinical Operations pool.

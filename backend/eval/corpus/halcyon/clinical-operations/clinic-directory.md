## What "hub clinic" means

Hub clinics hold the Tier 2 vaccine and injectable stock described in the in-clinic formulary, and act as the backup site for patients when a nearby clinic has to close unexpectedly. Non-hub clinics order from the nearest hub.

## Visit volumes

"Avg visits per day" is the in-person average for July–September 2026, taken from ClearPath PM. Each clinic's staffing band under the clinic staffing standard is set from this column every quarter, so a clinic can move bands when its volume changes. The telehealth front desk is staffed separately and has no band.

## Notes

- Riverside (RVS) and Riverbend (RVB) are different clinics about four miles apart. So are Northgate (NGT) and Northbridge (NBR). Always check the code on referrals, lab requisitions and courier pickups.
- Every clinic has at least one exam room accessible to wheelchair users and a height-adjustable exam table, as required by the patient intake procedure.
- All sites close on Halcyon's paid holidays. The telehealth front desk stays open 08:00–16:00 on holidays.
- If a site has to close unexpectedly (weather, power, staffing), the clinic manager tells the telehealth front desk supervisor so in-person visits can be converted to telehealth where clinically appropriate, and posts the closure on the Halcyon app.
- The clinic manager is the escalation point for a site when no charge nurse is on shift.

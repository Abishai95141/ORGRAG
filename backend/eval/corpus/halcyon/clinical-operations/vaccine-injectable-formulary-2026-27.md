# Halcyon in-clinic vaccine and injectable formulary (2026–27)

**Owner:** Pharmacy and Therapeutics subcommittee · **Clinical pharmacist lead:** Dr. Farah Qureshi, PharmD · **Effective:** September 15, 2026

This formulary lists the vaccines and injectable medications Halcyon clinics give on site. Anything not on this list cannot be given in clinic without approval from the Pharmacy and Therapeutics subcommittee. Insured patients are billed to their plan; the self-pay price applies to uninsured and self-pay patients and includes the administration fee.

## Tiers

- **Tier 1 — stocked at every clinic.** Give same day.
- **Tier 2 — stocked at hub clinics only** (see the hub column in the clinic directory). Other clinics request a transfer from the nearest hub; transfers arrive within 2 business days.
- **Tier 3 — not stocked.** Ordered for a named patient from the wholesaler; allow 5 business days.

## Cold chain

Vaccine refrigerators must stay between **2 °C and 8 °C**. Readings are logged automatically every 15 minutes and checked by the MA opening the clinic.

If a refrigerator reads outside 2–8 °C:

1. Do not discard anything. Label the affected stock "Do not use", keep it refrigerated in a working unit, and record the reading, time and duration.
2. Call the clinical pharmacist within 1 hour. The pharmacist checks with the manufacturers whether the stock can still be used.
3. File a SafeReport (Level 4 near miss if no vaccine was given; higher if a patient received affected vaccine).
4. Give no vaccine from the affected stock until the clinical pharmacist releases it in writing.

Frozen vaccines are never refrozen or reused once thawed.

## Ordering

Clinic managers order Tier 1 stock weekly from the formulary order form. Tier 2 transfers and Tier 3 named-patient orders go to the clinical pharmacist.

# Halcyon language access report — Q3 2026

**Prepared by:** Clinical Operations · **Period:** July 1 – September 30, 2026 · **Source:** Polyglen monthly quality reports and ClearPath PM visit data

This report covers interpreted visits delivered through Polyglen, in clinic and in telehealth visits. It is the first quarterly report in this format; earlier quarters were reported monthly by Polyglen and are not restated here.

## Connect-time targets

Polyglen's service levels set two tiers:

- **2-minute tier:** the top 20 languages and American Sign Language. Target: interpreter connected within 2 minutes.
- **5-minute tier:** all other languages. Target: interpreter connected within 5 minutes.

Connect time is measured from the clinician pressing "Request interpreter" to the interpreter joining. Averages are shown as minutes:seconds.

## Observations

- Spanish is 59% of all sessions and connects fastest.
- Dropped calls are highest for the smaller 5-minute-tier languages, where a dropped call often means waiting for the same interpreter to become free again.
- Telehealth visits accounted for 41% of interpreted minutes. Three-way calls in Juniper testing used the same Polyglen guest-link flow, so we expect this share to rise after the pilot starts.
- Front desk staff reported 23 cases where a family member offered to interpret. In every case staff used Polyglen instead, as the patient intake procedure requires outside emergencies.

## Patient feedback

Polyglen received 9 patient complaints in the quarter, 6 about wait times and 3 about audio quality. None involved interpreter conduct.

## Next steps

- Clinical Operations will raise every language that missed its connect-time target at the Polyglen renewal discussion in November 2026.
- Clinic managers should remind staff that ASL is available by video 24/7, including weekends at Harbor Point.
- The Q4 2026 report is due in mid-January 2027.

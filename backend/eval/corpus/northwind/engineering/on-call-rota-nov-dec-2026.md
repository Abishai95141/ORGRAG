## Handover

The outgoing primary posts a handover note in `#oncall-handover` by Tuesday 10:00: open incidents, holds or silenced alerts, and anything flaky. The incoming primary acknowledges it in the thread. Secondaries read it too; they get paged if the primary doesn't acknowledge within 10 minutes.

## Holiday cover

- Thanksgiving week: US-based engineers on the rota may hand overnight hours (22:00–07:00 Pacific) to the London-based follow-the-sun cover for that week. Arrange it with your team lead.
- The December 22 week falls in the holiday change freeze. Only standard and emergency changes ship; expect a quiet week but stay reachable.

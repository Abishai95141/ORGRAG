# Sales desk memo: discount authority from October 1, 2026

**From:** Marcus Bell (CRO) and Renata Kowal (CFO)
**Date:** September 24, 2026
**Effective:** quotes issued on or after October 1, 2026

## Summary

This memo replaces the "Discount authority" table in the Pricing and discount policy. Everything else in that policy is unchanged: list prices, the 15% discount for annual prepayment on Growth, the automatic 50% non-profit and education discount, refunds, and the list of things sales may never offer.

Two things are new. Approval limits now differ between Growth and Enterprise deals, and every discount above 15% goes through the new **deal desk** (run by Priscilla Okoye in sales operations) before it reaches the approver.

## Approval matrix

Discounts are still measured against the first-year contract value at list price.

| Discount off list | Growth deals | Enterprise deals | Deal desk review | Target turnaround |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Up to 10% | Account executive | Account executive | No | Same day |
| Over 10%, up to 15% | Account executive | Regional sales director | No | 1 business day |
| Over 15%, up to 25% | Regional sales director | Regional sales director | Yes | 1 business day |
| Over 25%, up to 35% | VP of Sales | VP of Sales | Yes | 2 business days |
| Over 35%, up to 45% | CRO | CRO and CFO jointly | Yes | 3 business days |
| Over 45% | Not offered | CRO and CFO jointly, CEO informed | Yes | 5 business days |

## Rules that have not changed

- Multi-year deals may add an extra 5% per committed year, capped at 10%. The multi-year extra does not count toward the thresholds above.
- The 50% non-profit and education discount on Growth is automatic and is not a negotiated discount, so it needs no approval.
- Custom data retention beyond the standard period still needs the security team, whatever the discount.

## How to submit

1. Build the quote in the CRM with the discount on the first-year line.
2. If the discount is over 15%, the quote is routed to the deal desk automatically. The deal desk checks margin, payment terms and any non-standard clauses, then forwards it to the approver in the matrix.
3. The approver records the decision in the CRM. Email and Slack approvals do not count.

Approvals are valid for 30 days. A quote that changes by more than 2 percentage points needs fresh approval.

## Why we changed it

Enterprise deals made up most of the discounts above 20% in Q2 and Q3, and the VP of Sales was the bottleneck for deals that regional directors understood better. At the same time, Growth deals are small enough that account executives can be trusted with a little more room.

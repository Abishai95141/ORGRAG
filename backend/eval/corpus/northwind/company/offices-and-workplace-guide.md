# Northwind offices and workplace guide

**Owner:** People team (Joanne Pryce, VP People) · **Last updated:** September 14, 2026

Most of Northwind works remotely most of the time. We keep three offices and a coworking membership for people who want a desk, and we use them for team weeks, customer visits and onboarding. This guide covers where they are, when they're open and how to use them.

## Booking a desk

Post in your office's channel (`#office-sf`, `#office-nyc`, `#office-london`, `#office-lisbon`) by the day before. Lisbon hot desks are first come, first served, but the membership only covers six people at once, so post anyway.

## Visitors and badges

Guests must be registered with the office lead at least one business day ahead and accompanied the whole time. Customers visiting for meetings get a visitor badge at reception. Candidates coming for onsite interviews are registered by the recruiter.

Badge access for employees is set up by IT during onboarding. Lost badges are reported in `#security-help` the same as lost laptops.

## Working from the office

- Guest Wi-Fi is for guests only. Employees use the managed network, which requires the endpoint agent on your laptop.
- Never leave a laptop unlocked at a hot desk. Screen lock policy is the same as at home.
- Whiteboards in meeting rooms are wiped every evening. Don't write customer names or anything Confidential on whiteboards in the Lisbon coworking space, which is shared with other companies.

## Team weeks

Teams can book a full office for a team week up to twice a year. Travel to team weeks is booked through Navan and follows the expense and travel policy. Ask your office lead for availability before booking travel.

## Coworking elsewhere

If you live more than 50 miles from an office and want a coworking desk, you can expense up to $250 per month with manager approval. Submit it in Ramp like any other expense.

## Questions

Ask in `#ask-anything` or message the office lead for your city.

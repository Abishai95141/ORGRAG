Generated by `backend/eval/verify_claims.py` from the committed result files. Every

## The headline run

150 questions over two organizations; Claude Haiku 5.5 answering in quick mode; judged
by `nvidia/nemotron-3-ultra-550b-a55b`; corpus digest `55fb424818e93128`;
settings `{"rerank": true, "recency_bias": "low", "model": "claude-haiku-5-5"}`. Recall@5 comes from the retrieval run on the
same corpus (`eval/results/retrieval-default.json`, 135 answerable questions).

| Claim | Published | Recomputed from rows | Matches run summary | How it's counted |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Answers judged correct | 94% | 141/150 = 94.0% | yes (0.94) | every key fact judged present and no contradiction; for unanswerable questions, a correct abstention |
| Faithfulness to the cited sources | 0.955 | 1157/1211 = 0.955 | yes (0.955) | claims supported by the retrieved passages / all claims |
| Citation precision | 0.98 | 871/887 = 0.982 | yes (0.982) | citations whose passage supports the statement / all citations on claims |
| Retrieval recall@5, reranked | 0.94 | mean of 135 = 0.937 | yes (0.937) | share of gold documents in the top 5, per answerable question |
| Mean time to answer | 5.4 s | 5.45 s (p95 8.5 s) | yes (5.4) | wall-clock seconds per answer, request to final token |
| Cost per answer | about $0.001 | mean $0.00114, median $0.00103 | yes (0.00114) | 1,310,798 input + 80,834 output tokens over 320 model calls; at $0.1/$0.5 per million that is $0.1715 for 150 answers |

71 of 150 answers cost under $0.001; the most expensive cost $0.00266. The run
was made before prompt caching, and 150 of its 150 answers completed.

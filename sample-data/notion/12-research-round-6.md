# Research round 6: findings

Researcher: Elin Hughes. 8 participants: 5 recreational boat owners and 3 commercial operators. 2 used screen readers and 1 used voice control.

## What we learned

1. 5 of 8 couldn't find the berth length field on mobile; it was below the vessel photo upload.
2. Commercial operators want to apply for several vessels at once. Today they have to repeat the whole journey for each.
3. Both screen reader users lost their place when the error summary appeared, because it doesn't link to the fields.
4. Nobody understood "pay later" without help, which supports dropping it.

## What we're changing

| Finding | Change | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Berth length hard to find | Move it above the photo upload and add a hint | Grace Liu |
| Several vessels | Explore a "add another vessel" pattern for round 8 | Tom Okafor |
| Error summary | Link each error to its field (also an audit failure) | Sam Doyle |

> Quote from a participant: "I knew my berth length, I just couldn't see where to put it."

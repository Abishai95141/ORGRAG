# On-call and incident process

One person is on call each week, Monday 09:00 to Monday 09:00. The rota is announced in #general and swaps are agreed in #incidents.

## Severity

| Severity | Meaning | Acknowledge within | Updates |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | The service is down or data is at risk | 15 minutes, day or night | Every 30 minutes |
| 2 | A large part of the service is failing | 30 minutes in working hours, 1 hour otherwise | Every hour |
| 3 | Degraded, with a workaround | Next working day | Daily |

## During an incident

1. Post in #incidents with the incident number (INC-year-number) and what users see.
2. Name an incident lead and someone on comms.
3. Put up the status banner if users are affected.
4. Write the timeline in the thread as you go.

## After an incident

Severity 1 and 2 incidents get a blameless postmortem in Notion within 5 working days, with actions, owners and due dates.

## Paid on-call

On-call pays £150 a week plus £40 for each call-out outside working hours.

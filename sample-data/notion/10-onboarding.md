# Onboarding checklist

Welcome to Harbourline Digital. Your line manager goes through this with you in your first week.

## Day 1

- [ ] Collect your laptop and pass from Pier House reception (3rd floor from 1 December)
- [ ] Ask Marcus Feld for GitHub, AWS and Slack access
- [ ] Join #harbourline-general, #harbourline-eng-standards and your team's channel

## Week 1

- [ ] Read the GDS Way and the Service Standard
- [ ] Read the engineering decision log
- [ ] Shadow a show and tell
- [ ] Complete the data protection training (about 45 minutes)

## Month 1

- [ ] Ship a change to production with a buddy
- [ ] Join the on-call rota as a shadow for one week
- [ ] Book a 30-minute session with Elin Hughes on how we do user research

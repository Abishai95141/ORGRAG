# purpose: Beacon: our internal AI assistant pilot over the handbook, standards and Slack.
Jonah Price: Beacon pilot kick-off. 40 staff for 8 weeks, starting Monday 3 November 2026. Success means 20 minutes saved per person per day and at least 80% of answers rated correct in the weekly sample. The cross-government M365 Copilot experiment reported 26 minutes a day, so 20 is deliberately conservative.

Priya Raman: We're mapping Beacon against the AI Playbook's 10 principles. The panel will push hardest on principle 4, meaningful human control. Beacon never sends anything on its own: it drafts, and a person decides.
Jonah Price: We had three options for the platform: Lighthouse (SaaS), Keel (open source, self-hosted) and building our own on our cloud account. Decision on 14 October: Keel for the pilot, because the data stays in our account and answers come with citations. We'll revisit in week 6.
  Ruth Adeyemi: Agreed. Please put the cost comparison in the Notion brief.
